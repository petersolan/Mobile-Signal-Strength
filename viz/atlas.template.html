Reading this data

  • Readings come from roof-mounted antennas on the vans with no correction for antenna gain. A phone inside a car or a building will see weaker signal.
  • The vans drive for other work, not coverage surveys, so only roads they used are covered: 8 hours in Oct–Nov 2022, about 1 hour in Sep 2023 and 3 hours in Jul 2024 (4G).
  • Thresholds follow Ofcom: 4G outdoor −105 dBm and indoor/in-car −95 dBm; 5G high confidence −110 dBm and very high confidence −100 dBm (SS-RSRP).
  • 5G for O2 and Vodafone is missing: the scanner was not tuned to their 5G channels in the Exeter passes. That does not mean they have no 5G here.
  • Each cell value is a median of per-pass medians, so a van parked with the scanner running counts once. One such stop logged about 480,000 5G readings in 2022.
  • In 3D view, column height is the signal level above the weakest value on the chart axis, exaggerated 14 m per dB. Colour carries the same value, so the flat view loses nothing.
  • Basemap roads, rail, water and parks are from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), bundled into the page.
  • The grid is also saved as data/derived/exeter_grid_100m.gpkg (EPSG:27700) for QGIS.
